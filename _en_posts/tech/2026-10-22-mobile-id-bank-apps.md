Mobile IDs can now be issued in bank apps as well
On October 13, 2026, the Ministry of the Interior and Safety announced that issuance had opened in the apps of IBK, Shinhan, Woori, and Hana Bank

**From October 14**, you can get one in the four banks' apps and use it right away

![Ministry of the Interior and Safety wordmark](/assets/images/tech/mobile-id-bank-apps/01-mois-wordmark-color.webp)
*Ministry of the Interior and Safety wordmark*

## Bank apps that started issuing mobile IDs

| Bank | App | Start date |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| IBK Industrial Bank | i-ONE Bank | October 14 |
| Shinhan Bank | Shinhan SuperSOL | October 14 |
| Woori Bank | Woori Bank app | October 14 |
| Hana Bank | **Hana 1Q** | October 14 |

Bank apps can issue <mark>5 types</mark> of ID
Resident registration cards, driver's licenses, national veterans' registration cards, alien registration cards, and disability registration cards

In an October 20 announcement, IBK said it covers 7 types, including permanent residence cards and domestic residence report cards
Without installing another app, you can issue, view, and present the ID right inside the existing **i-ONE Bank**

The four banks passed the ministry's **suitability assessment**
It is an assessment that verifies systems for blocking hacking and preventing personal data leaks

## How to get one

| Method | What you need | If you change phones |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Tap an IC ID card | IC ID card | Tap again |
| QR at an office visit | Visit a community service center | **Visit again** |
| Simple authentication | Some existing users | - |

New applicants have to **tap an IC ID card on their phone** or visit a designated office
Some users who already used a mobile ID on Samsung devices can use it <mark>with simple authentication alone</mark>

With the office visit method, your identity is checked first at the community service center
Then you scan a one-time QR code with the bank app

In a Monthly Review (Wolyo Shinmun) hands-on article, issuance took less than 3 minutes

The QR method costs nothing
Reports say that without an IC resident registration card, the reissue fee and IC chip cost together come to <u>₩10,000</u>

Issuing and reissuing a mobile driver's license in the government app was covered in a September 5 post

## 11 apps that can issue it

![Number of apps that can issue mobile IDs](/assets/images/tech/mobile-id-bank-apps/en/02-chart.webp)
*Number of apps that can issue mobile IDs — Source: self-rendered based on the Ministry of the Interior and Safety's announcement*

As of May 2026, issuance was available in the government app **Republic of Korea Mobile ID** and 6 private apps
The private apps are from Samsung Electronics, KB Kookmin Bank, Naver, NH NongHyup Bank, Toss, and KakaoBank

With the 4 banks added on October 14, it can now be issued in <mark>11 apps in total</mark>
If Samsung Card's **monimo** gets its system ready and passes the assessment, 12 apps will issue it from 2027

KakaoBank launched the service in July 2025 and had issued more than 700,000 by the end of August 2026
Supported apps differ by device and operating system, so check support on the issuance screen first

## Where it can be used

![Concept shot of presenting an ID on a phone](/assets/images/tech/mobile-id-bank-apps/03-agy.webp)
*Concept shot of presenting an ID on a phone — Source: concept shot · self-generated with agy*

A mobile ID has **the same legal effect** as a physical ID
It can be used for identity checks when opening bank accounts and at hospitals, government offices, domestic airports, and convenience stores
