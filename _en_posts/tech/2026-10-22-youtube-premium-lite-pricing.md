Nine months have passed since YouTube Premium Lite launched in Korea
At **₩8,500** a month, it removes ads from most videos and does not include YouTube Music

If you also want music, <mark>Premium costs less than subscribing to Lite and Music separately</mark>

*YouTube wordmark*

## Three plans

![Monthly prices of YouTube paid memberships](/assets/images/tech/youtube-premium-lite-pricing/en/02-chart.webp)
*Monthly prices of YouTube paid memberships — Source: self-rendered based on the official YouTube blog*

| Plan | Ad removal | YouTube Music |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Premium Lite | Most non-music videos | Not included |
| Music Premium | Music app only | **Included** |
| Premium | All videos and music | Included |

YouTube announced the Korean launch of **Premium Lite** on its official blog on January 30, 2026
All prices are for web and Android billing

Lite is a plan launched under a consent decision by the Korea Fair Trade Commission
The issue was the structure of selling YouTube Music bundled with Premium

Premium went up from ₩10,450 to ₩14,900 in December 2023
The gap between Lite and Premium is ₩6,400 a month, or <mark>₩76,800</mark> a year

## What the Lite plan does not include

Lite's ad removal applies only to <mark>most non-music content</mark>
Music content such as music videos is not covered

Lite benefits do not apply to **Shorts**
Ads may also appear on search and browse screens

Background play and offline downloads are included in Lite too
They work on phones, laptops, and TVs

Paying for Premium Lite and Music Premium separately comes to ₩20,490 a month
That is <u>₩5,590 more</u> than Premium

## iPhone billing is priced separately

Many users report being charged **₩10,900 a month** for Lite when paying in the YouTube app on iPhone
The official blog prices are for web and Android

With the same Google account, signing up on the web still works on iPhone
If you paid in the app, cancel in <u>Apple's subscription settings</u> first, then sign up again on the web

## Why signing up through India gets blocked

![Concept shot of matching the subscription country against the access location](/assets/images/tech/youtube-premium-lite-pricing/03-agy.webp)
*Concept shot of matching the subscription country against the access location — Source: concept shot · self-generated with agy*

A method of using Premium much more cheaply by signing up with an Indian account has long been shared
In August and September 2026, many reported auto-renewal being turned off and repeated payment errors

The YouTube Premium terms require that <mark>you use it in the country where you signed up</mark>
Misstating your sign-up country or mainly using it in another country violates the terms

The help center says the subscription may be canceled if you misrepresent your location with a **VPN** or similar
The terms also spell out measures such as suspending the YouTube account

Many report that it is hard to keep paying with a Korean card
There are also explanations that new subscriptions on the Indian app store mainly go through UPI auto-debit

## When traveling abroad

If you travel to a country where Premium is supported, the benefits stay as they are
In countries where the service is not supported, the benefits cannot be used

Videos downloaded for offline viewing before leaving can be watched for **30 days**
If you stay abroad for more than 30 days or move, there may be <mark>measures such as canceling the subscription</mark>
