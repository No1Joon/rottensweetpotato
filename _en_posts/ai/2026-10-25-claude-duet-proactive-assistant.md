On October 21, Anthropic released **Claude Duet**, an assistant app that contacts the user first
It checks information in connected apps every few hours and reaches out when something needs doing

As a research preview, though, it is only available to Pro and Max subscribers in the <mark>US</mark> after joining a waitlist

*Set of Claude connector icons on the Claude Duet announcement page — Source: Anthropic*

## What was announced

![Official Anthropic logo](/assets/images/ai/claude-duet-proactive-assistant/02-logo-anthropic.webp)
*Official Anthropic logo*

Claude Duet is a **separate app** installed apart from the Claude chat app
It is available as iPhone and Android apps and on claude.ai

The model is fixed to **Claude Opus 5.5**
Duet sets the reasoning effort level for each task automatically

Anthropic set its main target users as professionals and <mark>small business owners</mark>
It gave examples such as scheduling meetings, building dashboards, forecasting sales, and prototyping web apps

- Picks important items from connected apps and gives a briefing every morning
- Checks accounts every few hours and messages only when needed
- Handles tasks given by text or voice in the background
- Works with its own virtual machine and browser, continuing even when the phone is off

## How it works and its limits

![Concept shot of organized task cards rising above a phone each morning](/assets/images/ai/claude-duet-proactive-assistant/03-agy.webp)
*Concept shot of organized task cards rising above a phone each morning — Source: concept shot · self-generated with agy*

Duet automatically looks up material, writes drafts, and prepares tasks
Before any action that affects the outside world, it <u>shows exactly what it will do and asks for approval</u>

| Type | Actions |
| :--- | :--- |
| Goes ahead on its own | Reading, drafting, preparing |
| Goes ahead after approval | **Sending, paying, signing, deleting** |
| Not allowed even with approval | <mark>Securities trading, bank transfers</mark> |

It also cannot call or text other people
Only emails sent from the connected mail account are handled, with approval

### 🔐 Sensitive information

A new classifier checks every message for sensitive information
Top-tier information such as card numbers and ID numbers is <mark>blocked</mark>

Health and personal financial information is sent only when the user explicitly instructs it
Passwords are taken in a **separate input window** on the device, designed so they are not stored in the conversation history

### 💳 Payments

Purchases go only through Stripe's wallet **Link** or Shopify's **Shop Pay**
Each purchase uses a single-use virtual card, so the real card number is not passed to the seller

### 🔌 Connected apps

| Type | Services |
| :--- | :--- |
| Mail, calendar | Gmail, Google Calendar |
| Documents, collaboration | Google Drive, Slack |
| Business | **QuickBooks, HubSpot** |
| Contracts, booking | Docusign, Calendly |

Microsoft 365 cannot be connected yet
On sites with no connector, Duet does the work with its cloud browser

### ⚠️ Limits

Because it checks accounts at set intervals, it <mark>may not respond right away to urgent changes</mark> in between
Anthropic also said it may misjudge what is urgent

Users cannot view or take over the browser screen Duet is using

## Pricing and usage

![Bar chart of the lowest monthly price for using a proactive agent](/assets/images/ai/claude-duet-proactive-assistant/en/04-chart.webp)
*Bar chart of the lowest monthly price for using a proactive agent — Source: self-rendered based on Anthropic and OpenAI price lists*

During the research preview, Duet is included in the Pro and Max plans **at no extra cost**
Pro is $20 (₩28,000) a month, and Max starts at $100 (₩140,000) a month

In exchange, it shares the same <u>5-hour and weekly usage limits</u> as Claude chat
Background work such as morning briefings and scheduled checks also counts against the limits

Anthropic gives approved users a one-time <mark>full reset</mark> of their limits
This reset can be used until November 30, 2026

## Conditions for use in Korea

During the research preview, <mark>it cannot be used outside the US</mark>

Team and Enterprise plans are not covered yet either
Anthropic said it plans to offer it on those two plans later

The connected app list has **no Korean services**
Few services are available for Korean small business owners who use KakaoTalk, Naver Mail, or Smart Store
